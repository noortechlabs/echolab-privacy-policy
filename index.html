EchoLab - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EchoLab - Naat Khwani App (“EchoLab”, “the app”, “we” or “our”), published by NoorTechLabs, handles information when you use the Android app.

1. Summary

  • EchoLab is a free audio tool. It lets you hear your voice live through headphones or speakers with adjustable delay, echo, reverb and tone effects, and lets you record the processed sound.
  • All audio processing happens on your device. The app does not send your microphone audio, your recordings, your settings or any usage information to NoorTechLabs or to anyone else.
  • The app does not request the Android Internet permission, has no accounts or login, and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

2. Information we collect and share

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share personal information. EchoLab has no server. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any account details.

The app includes one third-party component, the open-source Oboe audio library from Google, which runs entirely on your device to provide low-latency audio. It does not send data anywhere.

Your device’s operating system and any connected audio hardware (for example a Bluetooth headset or a USB-C audio device) handle audio on your device as part of normal Android operation. Their use of information is governed by their own providers’ policies.

3. Microphone access and live monitoring

EchoLab asks for microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) so it can capture sound from the input you select and play it back, with the effects you choose, through the output you select. This is the app’s core function.

The microphone is used only after you start live monitoring (the START button), and recording (the REC button) works only while monitoring is running. After you start monitoring, EchoLab can keep monitoring while the screen is off or while you use another app, so you can keep using it while performing. While this runs, Android shows an ongoing notification (“EchoLab is monitoring”). You can stop monitoring at any time from the app or by opening the notification. EchoLab never starts the microphone unless you have pressed START.

4. Recording and local storage

If you press REC, EchoLab records the processed sound (what you hear, including effects) and saves it as an M4A audio file on your device. On Android 10 and newer the file is saved in the Music/EchoLab folder. On older supported Android versions it is saved in the app’s own folder on external storage. The app does not need a storage permission for this.

Recordings stay on your device and are not uploaded. Because they are saved in your shared Music folder, other apps that you allow to access your audio files (for example a music player or file manager) may be able to see them.

5. Bluetooth, USB-C and wired audio

EchoLab can use the phone’s built-in microphone or a connected microphone, and can play to the phone speaker, wired headphones, USB-C audio devices or Bluetooth devices, depending on what your phone and accessories support. The app lists the audio devices your phone reports so you can choose them. It does not store a list of your devices or send it anywhere. The Bluetooth permission is used only to connect audio to a Bluetooth headset or speaker you choose.

6. Permissions and why they are used

PermissionPurpose
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)Capture your voice for live monitoring and for recordings you start.
Nearby devices / Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT)Use a Bluetooth headset or speaker for audio input or output.
Modify audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS)Route audio to the input and output you choose.
Foreground service and microphone foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE)Keep live monitoring that you started running with the screen off, with a visible notification.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)Show the ongoing “EchoLab is monitoring” notification.

EchoLab does not request Internet, location, camera, contacts, calendar, phone, SMS or storage permissions.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your settings and presets are saved only inside the app on your device. Android’s automatic app backup is turned off for EchoLab. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app removes the settings and presets.

Recordings are kept until you delete them. To delete a recording, use your phone’s Files, Music or Gallery app and open Music/EchoLab. On Android 10 and newer, recordings in the shared Music folder can remain after you uninstall EchoLab, so delete them separately if you no longer want them. Because NoorTechLabs holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Security

Because audio, recordings and settings are not uploaded, they are as secure as your device. Protect your phone with a screen lock and keep Android updated. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone or to your saved audio files may be able to listen to your recordings.

9. Children

EchoLab is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child’s use of the app, contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, for example by adding network access, analytics or cloud features, we will update this policy at this same address and change the effective date before releasing that version.
