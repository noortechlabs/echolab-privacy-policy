EchoLab - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EchoLab - Naat Khwani App (“EchoLab”, “the app”, “we” or “our”), published by NoorTechLabs, handles information when you use the app.

1. Summary

EchoLab is an audio monitoring and effects tool designed to help Naat reciters and other vocal users hear their voice live with adjustable audio effects and, when they choose, record the processed audio. Audio processing and recording are performed locally on your Android device. The app does not send your microphone audio or saved recordings to NoorTechLabs or to an app-operated server.

2. Microphone access and live monitoring

EchoLab requests microphone permission so it can capture audio from the input you select and route/process that audio to the output you select. This is the app’s core function. The microphone is used when you start live monitoring or recording. EchoLab may continue live monitoring while the screen is off or the app is not in the foreground after you start the feature. Android displays an ongoing foreground-service notification while background monitoring is active. You can stop monitoring from the app.

EchoLab does not intentionally activate the microphone for covert recording. Recording is a separate user-controlled feature and begins when you choose the REC control.

3. Audio effects and recording

EchoLab processes audio on your device to provide features such as live delay, feedback and mix controls, input/output gain, tone/EQ controls, mute, bypass and audio protection features. If you press REC, the app records the processed audio and saves it as an audio file on your device. On Android 10 and newer, recordings are saved in the Music/EchoLab folder. On older supported Android versions, the app uses app-specific external storage.

Recordings remain on your device under your control. You can delete them using your device’s file or audio-management tools. On newer Android versions, recordings saved in the shared Music folder may remain after you uninstall EchoLab; delete those files separately if you no longer want them.

4. Information we collect and share

Based on the app project reviewed for this policy, EchoLab does not require an account, does not ask you to provide a name, phone number or profile, and does not include app-operated analytics, advertising or diagnostic-reporting services. The project does not request Android internet permission and the app does not send audio, recordings, settings or usage information to NoorTechLabs or an app-operated server.

Microphone audio is accessed and processed locally to provide the function you request. Audio files you choose to record are stored locally. We do not receive or share those audio files.

Your Android operating system and any connected audio hardware or platform services may process information as necessary to provide device features such as microphone access, Bluetooth/audio routing, notifications and file storage. Those services are governed by the relevant platform or device provider’s terms and privacy information.

5. Permissions and why they are used

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): captures your voice/audio for live monitoring and recording.
  • Bluetooth / nearby devices: supports audio routing to or from compatible Bluetooth devices where available.
  • Modify audio settings: supports the app’s audio-routing and audio-control functions.
  • Foreground service and microphone foreground service: allows monitoring that you started to continue while the screen is off, subject to Android restrictions.
  • Notifications: displays the ongoing monitoring notification and related status.

Available input and output routes depend on your Android device, operating-system version and connected hardware.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

App settings and presets are stored locally on your device. Recordings are kept until you delete them. We do not maintain a remote copy. You can stop a recording, delete saved files from your device, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app may not remove recordings saved in the shared Music/EchoLab folder on newer Android versions.

7. Security

Because audio and settings are handled locally and are not uploaded to an EchoLab server, NoorTechLabs does not hold a remote copy of your recordings. Protect access to your device with its screen lock and keep Android updated. Anyone who has access to your unlocked device or its saved audio files may be able to access those files.

8. Children and users of different ages

EchoLab is intended as a general-purpose vocal audio utility for users across age groups and is not built around child-directed content or child-specific profiles. It does not provide accounts or collect a child's personal information through an account system. A parent or guardian should supervise a minor’s use of microphone and recording features where appropriate. If the app is declared on Google Play as targeting children, including users under 13, the developer must ensure that the app, its SDKs and its distribution comply with Google Play’s Families requirements and applicable law before selecting those age groups.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app’s features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at the same privacy-policy address with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
